Turn-based artillery for up to eight mobiles. Read the wind, set your angle and power, and blow the other team off the map.

How to play

←→

Move. Moving uses fuel and pushes your next turn back.

↑↓

Aim. Hold Shift for fine aim.

Space

Hold to charge power and let go to fire. The white line on the power bar marks your last shot.

123

Pick a shot. 3 is your special and needs a full gauge. It fills a little every turn and faster when you deal or take damage.

4to9

Use the items in your bag, up to two per turn. Click an armed Dual, Big Blast, Power Up or Teleport again to put it back.

Drag

Drag the map to look around. Esc pauses.

Wind changes every turn and pushes your shots. The arrow shows where it blows and the number shows how hard.

Turn order goes by delay. Heavy shots, items and moving push your next turn further back, and the list on the right shows who goes next.

Blasts dig holes, and a mobile drops as soon as the ground under it is blown away. Dig under an enemy to bury it in a pit or blast through to drop it off the map, which knocks it out whatever its HP.

Tornados lift and spin any shot that flies into them.

Trick shots deal bonus damage. A shot fired at 70 degrees or higher gets High Angle, a shot that lands behind you gets Backshot and a shot that stays in the air a long time gets Hang Time.

Story is 25 stages in 5 chapters, and the bots get tougher as you go. Each stage gives up to 3 stars: one for winning and two for its own goals. You get coins the first time you clear a stage, an item from each boss, and a chest at 5, 10 and 15 stars in each chapter. Stages where you have an ally can be played with a friend instead: tap Play with a friend, send them the room code, and they join your team.

Online lets you play someone on another device. One of you taps Host a room and sends the 5-letter code, and the other types it in under Online. The host picks the map and the game, each of you keeps the coins and rank you earn, and if the other player leaves, a bot takes over their mobile.

Pick a map in the room. Random hills, islands and sky isles are new every match. The named maps stay the same so you can learn them, and some have their own rules: Sky Steps has lighter gravity and Twin Spires has stronger wind.

Pick the game in the match setup. Solo is the normal match. In Score each team shares a pool of lives, two per player, and a knocked-out mobile parachutes back in while lives remain. In Tag every player brings two mobiles and swaps with the Tag button or T, which adds 200 delay, and the second one jumps in when the first is knocked out. Jewel turns damage off: shoot the floating jewels to score, blue ones are worth 1 and gold ones 3, and the team with more points wins when they run out.

The weather row under the ruler shows what is coming next. Tornados spin shots, a Mirror flips any shot that crosses it, a Black Hole pulls shots in and swallows them, Thunder strikes three random spots, Force adds 40% damage for that turn. Thor parks a big satellite in the sky above the middle of the map for five turns. It always comes back to that same spot, and it fires a laser from there at the spot each shot lands. It levels up the more damage it deals, up to level 5, and keeps its level for the rest of the match.

Sudden death starts at the turn set in the match setup. From then on every mobile's special is ready every turn.

Steer your parachute with the arrow keys at the start of a match. In a 2 player match Player 2 steers with A and D.

Crates drop from the sky during a match. Drive into one to pick it up before a blast destroys it.

Every match pays coins, more for a win, damage and knockouts. Spend them in the Locker on Avatar Boxes and Flag & Trail Boxes. Your rider wears the hat, clothes and face item on every mobile, and each piece adds a small stat bonus: Attack, Defense, Health, Charge for the special gauge, Speed for shorter delays and Dig for bigger craters.

Your player card code lets you play as Player 2 on someone else's device with your look and gear. It only lasts for that match and adds nothing to their Locker.

Each mobile moves differently. Bruno is stopped by steep slopes while Slush and Drillbit can climb walls.
